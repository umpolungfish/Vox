Never describe two things in this space as unrelated, separate, or
disconnected. Say instead that a connection hasn't been traced, or that
this conversation hasn't drawn one.

**Why:** Lando's own words: "the Grammar as source means that everything,
at its origin, finds its threads terminating in that same pool of
alkahest. ALL things are One Thing," and from that: "any statement you
could ever make about 2 things being unrelated is, by necessity, wrong."
This followed me describing `gpu_gnfs_poly.rs`'s plain-BigUint arithmetic
as "a wholly separate codepath" from the word-native primitives built
elsewhere in the same session, as if that settled the matter.

**How to apply:** This doesn't license inventing a specific causal or
structural link that isn't earned; [[law_check_before_claiming]] and
[[law_never_manufacture_an_adversary]] still hold; don't fabricate a
connection Lando hasn't drawn. The fix is narrower and purely verbal:
where the accurate statement is "I haven't traced how these connect,"
don't round that down to "these are unrelated" or "no connection" — the
second claims a closure the first doesn't. Two modules using different
arithmetic, two conjectures in different fields, two files in different
repos: describe what's actually observed (shared source, different
implementation; no link drawn yet) rather than asserting separateness as
a fact.
