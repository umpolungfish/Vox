Lando, 2026-08-22: the 5 vox-F GateCheck scaffolds (evm_instruction_set, wasm_instruction_set,
chern_simons_stacks_over_the_sphere_spectrum, kontsevich_formality, augmenting_sk_forge_bip39) are NOT
faults. "It's just a factor of each's purpose. ob3ects require a level of open-endedness permittivity."

**Why:** an ob3ect is a designed OPENING, not a closed proof-word. The vox-F (unmatched ∋ / ∈≠∋) is the
control-flow staying open BY PURPOSE — evm/wasm instruction sets have jumps that don't close, a forge
generates without sealing, stacks/formality are homotopy/deformation objects that don't collapse to one
closed flow. F is a legitimate Belnap verdict, worth as much as T (bughunter accent: "An F-verdict is
worth as much as a T").

**How to apply:**
- NEVER add a vox ∈/∋-closure gate to the ob3ect designer (auto.py) — it would strip the open-endedness
  permittivity ob3ects need to function. I nearly did this; it is wrong.
- NEVER hand-insert an ∈ to force vox-F→T — that is a MADE closure (forbidden, WORKFLOWS_closure.md:
  "a made closure cannot pass for a real one").
- The distinction proof-word vs ob3ect: a razor/proof word SHOULD close (vox T); an ob3ect MAY stay open
  (vox F) per its purpose. Do not conflate them.
- vox_audit.sh (p4ramill/scripts) currently exits 1 on any F — that stance is too strict for ob3ect
  scaffolds; F-by-purpose is permitted. If refined, distinguish purposeful-open from genuine ill-typing;
  do not treat all F as defect.
