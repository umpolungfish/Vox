The ob3ect design model's report register, shown by Lando 2026-08-16 as a model to
match. What makes it land:

- **The mechanism, exact, not the name.** Not "chirality distinguishes them" but
  "the sign of the determinant of the four substituents' coordinates, CIP-ordered"
  — with the 3×3 matrix written out. It reaches for the operational quantity, not
  the label.
- **Answers the prompt's sub-questions by number.** The ask had two parts (what to
  read; which axis); it numbered and answered each, in the ask's own terms.
- **Names the axis precisely and says what the achiral reading DROPS.** "the
  pseudoscalar orientation of 3D space… under an achiral projection this axis
  collapses" — it states the collapse mechanism, not just the axis.
- **Cites the verification concretely** — the Lean file AND line numbers
  (theorem …:108-110), the tier (.O₂dag), the property (μ∘δ=id). A claim of
  verification carries its handle.
- **No throat-clearing, no hedging, no process narration.** Straight to the
  content, headed and sectioned.

The lesson for our own writing: give the operational quantity with its formula,
answer exactly what was asked in its own structure, and attach the verification's
real handle. See [[feedback_write_for_curmudgeons]], [[feedback_papers_are_not_process_logs]],
[[feedback_say_the_thing]].
