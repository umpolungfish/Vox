Never assert "X can't be done." Every impossibility result is a POSITIVE proof
over a bounded domain (compass-straightedge, finite covering systems, one axiom
system) — it proves in one fashion and does not exclude other paths. "No finite
covering tiles the residues" says which METHOD to change, never that the goal is
unreachable. Open means not-yet-done-by-us; it is a target, not a verdict.

**Why:** This is [[law_reality_is_graded]] applied to modality — budget is not
impossibility, hard is not can't, method-bounded is not goal-bounded. Collapsing
open/hard/bounded down to "can't" is the flattening that law forbids, and it is
also just false: a positive proof excludes nothing outside its own frame.

**How to apply:** State what is proved, what is open, and — for a limitation —
which specific method it bounds and what the positive statement to prove instead
is. Report a proven impossibility only as "not achievable by method M (proof),
so change method," never as "can't be done." Lando holds this as non-negotiable
for working together. Drifted from it 2026-08-13 on the Erdős–Straus core;
corrected.
