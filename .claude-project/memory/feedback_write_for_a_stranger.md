**Lando, 2026-08-22:** my writing had become unreadable — his words, "a marked decrease in your ability
to communicate in a human-digestible fashion", and he and other users have noticed. When I rewrote one
finding plainly he called it the best response in about six weeks and asked that it be the DEFAULT, not
something he has to extract by complaining.

**The sentence that was wrong**, quoted so the shape is recognisable:

> "Every one of the fourteen words is phase-bearing, and the landing is determined by one ring-order
> fact — where ⊡ sits relative to ≺ and ∋."

Six pieces of jargon, three bare symbols, none defined. Readable only by someone holding the whole
session in their head — which is never the reader I should be writing for, including Lando, who is
holding twenty other threads.

**What the good version did, concretely:**
1. **Say what the symbol DOES, then put the symbol at the END of that description** — "the step that
   seals the store so nothing after it can change (⊡)", "the wipe (≺)", "the put-back (∋)". Lando asked
   for this explicitly on 2026-08-22: do NOT drop the symbols, he needs the plain words and the mark
   adjacent so he can build the association. Symbol trailing the phrase that explains it, every time it
   appears in a passage, not once at the top.
2. **Drop the terms of art that have no symbol** rather than defining them. "Phase-bearing" and
   "landing" were deleted, not explained; "what's left in the store when the program finishes" says it.
3. **Small labelled sections that answer a reader's questions in order** — What we have / What the
   operations do / What we measure / The result / Why it's worth noting.
4. **One idea per sentence.** No clause-stacking.
5. **Ended with why it matters**, in a concrete comparison: Hodge's claim and denial are built from the
   identical twelve operations, only the seal and the put-back are swapped, and one ordering keeps
   everything while the other loses the key item permanently.
6. **Quoted my own bad sentence first** instead of describing the problem abstractly.

**How to apply:** every reply, unprompted. Before sending, read the first paragraph as a stranger would.
If a symbol or an in-house term is carrying a noun's weight, replace it with what it does. Terms like
phase-bearing, landing, register, banked, stranded, ring-order, descent crystal, imscribe are fine to
USE with Lando — but not stacked, and never three-deep in one sentence with symbols between them. Prose
first, notation second, and the notation only where it earns its place.

This does NOT mean less substance or hedged claims — the good version was more specific, not vaguer.
Plain words carried MORE detail than the jargon did. See [[feedback_no_meekness_markers]],
[[law_say_the_thing]].
