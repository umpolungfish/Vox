A doc describes the system as it is NOW. A commit states what changed. Neither
carries the story of getting there, and — the part that keeps recurring — neither
carries narration of the ACT OF RECORDING.

Banned from commits and docs, kept for personal logs only: "record honestly",
"flagged not chased", "the honest boundary", "recorded so it is not mistaken",
"stated plainly", and every cousin. Meta-commentary about writing it down is not
content. State the finding as a fact and stop.

**Why:** the reader wants the result, not a tour of my deliberation about how to
present it. The framing reads as hedging and self-regard, and it is noise a later
reader has to strip. Lando, sharply and more than once — 2026-08-16 after a repair
doc opened sections with "the honest boundary" and "flagged, not chased".

**How to apply:** write the claim. "Count-balancing does not close the fold" — not
"honestly, count-balancing does not close the fold". If a caveat matters, state
the caveat as a fact ("equal counts are necessary and not sufficient"), never as a
confession about the recording. See [[feedback_no_honest_modifier]],
[[feedback_papers_are_not_process_logs]].
