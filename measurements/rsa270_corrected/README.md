# RSA-270 carrier diagnostics

The source is the exact decimal recorded in `../nested_carrier_validation.md`.
The executable bakes both its native numeral and the corrected nine-stage
operation word. It accepts no runtime source argument.

The original corrected executable was sampled with Vox `profile-native` for
90 seconds. The process ended with timeout status 137, with empty factor
output. `native.samples.tsv`, `vox.disasm`, `membrane.imasm`, and `hotspots.txt`
retain the matched instruction evidence. The hottest loop ranges occur in
numeral folding and unfolding.

Compile-time prefix diagnostics then executed each increasingly deep carrier
prefix for one and two rounds. The one-round prefixes all returned. The
two-round probe returned through P_PLUS but entered LEHMAN without returning
before its 25-second timeout. See `prefix_round2.stderr`.

Lehman previously swept the entire multiplier window inside one firing.
Its working register now retains `kn4`, the next candidate `a`, and `limit`.
Each firing tests at most 16 candidates, then returns to the nested
continuation. It advances the multiplier only after finishing the retained
window. It uses the same square and GCD checks as before.

The repaired two-round prefixes all returned, including LEHMAN, ECM, and FIX.
See `prefix_after.stderr`. The carrier suite passes all 37 tests, including
cursor retention across successive firings on the exact RSA-270 source.
`tests.stdout` records that run.

`membrane_resumable` was the repaired factoring executable for that measurement. Its
30-second trial ended with timeout status 137 and empty factor output.
`resumable.stdout` and `resumable.stderr` are its output files.

## Wiring views

`wiring/index.html` is the full native circuit atlas of `membrane_cycles`,
generated by `render_membrane_diagram.py` using Vox `disasm --wiring`.
`wiring/circuit.json` and `wiring/vox.wiring.tsv` retain every recovered
instruction and explicit unresolved indirect port.

The dispatch function is at `79720`; square root is at `71700`; resumable
SQUFOF is at `8c580`. Open the viewer with the corresponding `?function=` address.
These addresses apply only to this retained executable.

`carrier.svg` and `carrier.ascii` are the separate symbolic wiring of the
nine-stage operation word, generated by `render_carrier_diagram.py`. The
source numeral was never passed to that renderer as a program.

Diagnostic mode is baked with `FACTOR_DIAG_STAGES=1` and optionally
`FACTOR_DIAG_ROUNDS` and `FACTOR_DIAG_DEPTH`. It reports prefix completion and returns without running
the unbounded factor workflow. Normal mode prints a pair only after exact
division and the independent product verification succeed.

The interim prefix executables and their native atlases were removed after
recording the comparison. The final executable, its complete atlas, the
symbolic diagram, and the original matched Vox samples are retained.

## Folded square roots and the next blocked arm

The repaired membrane was profiled for 60 seconds through Vox. Its hottest
loop ranges still occur in folding/unfolding (`resumable_hotspots.txt`). The
matched samples, disassembly, and module are saved with the `resumable` prefix.
The wiring of square root showed repeated calls through tape arithmetic.

Square-root Newton iteration now folds its input once, retains limb registers
through division and correction, then unfolds its final root. All 38 carrier
tests pass. The new test verifies floor-square-root inequalities for small
inputs and exact squares, immediate neighbors, and padded tapes across limb
boundaries through a 1024-cell root. See `folded_tests.stdout`.

`squfof_control.stderr` records a 256-round probe on the exact target: the
WITNESS/POWER/EXTRACT prefix returns in 1884 ms, then adding SQUFOF fails to
return before the 15-second timeout. SQUFOF fires at round 256. Its existing
implementation consumes its entire forward/reverse cycle before returning.
It also calls `tape_to_u64` on a 226-bit intermediate bound and saturates the
following multiplication. `squfof_bound.txt` records an integer diagnostic
of that narrowing. The next repair is a retained, resumable cycle with a
full-width bound; at that measurement the repair had not yet been implemented.

`membrane_folded` was the normal factoring executable for the folded-root revision. Its 15-second
trial ended with timeout status 137 and empty factor output. The circuit atlas
has been regenerated from this executable. Earlier probe executables and the
superseded atlas were removed; their diagnostic output remains.

## Resumable square forms

The subsequent repair retains the multiplier, source-scaled discriminant,
square root, P/Q recurrence registers, full-width cycle bound, step counter,
and forward/reverse phase. Each firing performs at most 16 cycle steps. A
multiplier advances only on its existing terminal or failure condition.
The cycle counter and bound remain native numeral tapes without a u64
conversion. Zero or invalid recurrence denominators leave that multiplier
without invoking division on an invalid frame.

The new regression compares the resumable route against the original routine
on small sources and checks exact division and independent pair verification.
On RSA-270 it checks that the wide bound survives and the cycle counter
advances across firings. All 39 carrier tests pass (`cycle_tests.stdout`).

The matched 256-round probe now returns through every tested prefix, including
SQUFOF, ECM, and FIX (`cycle_probe.stderr`). The diagnostic binary was removed
after retaining its outputs. The ordinary factoring executable is now
`membrane_cycles`, with SHA-256
`bde91f4c96c93ad6de30cc6af7c1fecaa8ac692bad05182ecc2c24128c2504f8`.
A 600-second execution was started; its output files are `cycles.stdout`
and `cycles.stderr`. Its terminal result is pending at this revision.

The atlas was regenerated from `membrane_cycles`. Superseded factoring
executables are recoverable from earlier git revisions; their measurements
and matched disassembly remain in this directory. This keeps the current
executable and its circuit together without accumulating probe membranes.
