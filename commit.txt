Name the fallback-sweep F, not just the descent one

Applied vox to the G-mOMonadOS factoring binary and found F 2 in the
whole-image tally, with only one address ever printed. The descent loop's
illtyped list captured address and word for its own F; the fallback-sweep
flush closure counted sweep_f but threw the address and word away the
moment it found one. There was no way to see what the second F actually
was.

Gave the sweep loop the same illtyped capture the descent loop already
had: the flush closure now records the fragment's start address and its
glyph word when the verdict isn't T/B/N, capped at eight like the descent
list. The summary now prints both lists, each line tagged descent or
sweep, instead of stopping at whichever one already had a home.

Rebuilt and reinstalled, then ran it against target/release/g-momonados
again: the descent F is std::env::_var_os, standard library. The sweep F
is a fragment of g_momonados::loss::loss_main's cold panic path
(unwrap_failed, panic_bounds_check) — descent already claimed that
function's hot path, and the sweep picked up the orphaned tail as its own
fragment, which reads as ill-typed because a fragment cut mid-function
isn't a whole control-flow graph. Neither F touches any of the factoring
code (native_numeral, trilattice_factor, gpu_ecm, gpu_rho, gpu_rho_ml,
gpu_gnfs, winding_period) — checked directly by address, not assumed.
