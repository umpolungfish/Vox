Emit a binary that performs the factoring transformation

Mirrors perfect_one: factor_one.sh bakes N as its IMASM numeral, compiles the
factoring membrane against it, and the built artifact IS that one
transformation, run with no input. The membrane splits N with delta into its two
lanes and the core multiply-mu closes only on p*q = N, so the binary reads the
factor pair off the closure. ./factor_one.sh 8051 emits its binary and prints
factors 83 x 97. The reach is the 31-membrane's; the emitter is the perfect_one
pattern turned to factoring.
