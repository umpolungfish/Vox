Move Vox out of Python: a standalone Rust crate on the foundation

Vox is now a Rust crate that builds on its own — zero external crates, no
capstone, no pefile, and no mOMonadOS repo required. src/vox_decode.rs is the
native x86-64 decoder and ELF reader; src/vox.rs lifts a function's CFG to the
twelve-glyph IMASM word and verdicts its closure (T closes, B holds a fork open
across a terminal, N never forked, F ill-typed). src/main.rs is the CLI —
lift an ELF, verdict a word, classify a mnemonic, --selftest.

The two modules were the canonical Rust source living in mOMonadOS/src; they now
live here as the one home, and mOMonadOS links this crate rather than carrying a
copy. cargo build --release, then target/release/vox <file.so>. Verified: the
selftest passes and it lifts a real x86-64 .so natively.

The x86 lane — the only lane that executed in the Python — is moved. The EVM,
WASM, CPython and genetics lanes are still the Python vox.py until their lifters
are ported to Rust too.
