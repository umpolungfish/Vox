Execute factorization over nested IMASM numeral tapes

I added a Vox factor path whose input, live arithmetic state, candidates,
remainders, selection, and output are native IMASM numeral words. Each stage
is an operator word and the tower recursively executes phase generation,
arithmetic, branching, selection, continuation, and fixation.

I verified the mark-only path on composite and prime controls, including
143 = 11 x 13 and 8051 = 83 x 97.
