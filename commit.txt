I extend the baked membrane with a variable-radix register lift

I bake the phase base and lift radix as separate IMASM numeral words. The
prefix registers now close the running product modulo successive radix powers
using dynamic numeral tapes, and each step checks the generalized digit
equation. I verified radix 3 on 91 = 7 × 13 and tested radices through
2^80 + 1, alongside the full library test suite.
