verdict reads stdin, because the words that matter do not fit in argv

A lifted proof term runs to tens of thousands of glyphs — mu_delta_A_id is 86,065
— and an OS argument list will not carry that, so the words this verb is most
wanted for were exactly the ones it could not be handed. `vox verdict -` takes the
word on stdin and prints the verdict alone, which is also what a sweep wants when
piping thousands through.

Found by nesting the Lean corpus into Vox and having the shell refuse the first
interesting case.
