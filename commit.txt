The PDB reader accepts one-letter residue names

The odot and DARPin designs in red-hot_rebis name residues in one letter
(ATOM … N M A 1, resName M) where a standard PDB names them in three (MET). The
reader knew only three, so it read those files as empty and a sweep scored them a
blank word — seventeen of sixty-eight, which is a reader gap wearing the look of a
result. It now takes the three-letter reading where it maps and accepts a lone
alphabetic residue name as already one-letter, recovering all seventeen.

Confirmed against the sweep: the only blanks left are three copies of a poly-alanine
scaffold (antidote_fusion.pdb, all ALA), which genuinely carry no promoted residue
— a coordinate placeholder with no sequence, correctly read as no word rather than
forced into one.
