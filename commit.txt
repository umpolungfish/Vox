Give the braid factor path a real fold engine

The braid route resolved the multiplicative order by brute force and then
read the winding back off that same order, so it only ever closed the toy
moduli whose order is tiny. I put Brent's variant of Pollard's rho over the
numeral tapes in morphism_factor and route shor_factor_via_braid through it
first: the orbit y <- y^2+c on the residue ring is a winding, and a
non-trivial gcd of the accumulated branch differences against N is the
address where two branches fold onto one residue, which names a factor
without first resolving the whole order. rho_probe exposes it directly for
measurement. Verified on the small cases and on a balanced 64-bit semiprime;
the order/winding closure stays as the fallback arm.
