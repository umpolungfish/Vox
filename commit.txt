verdict --tsv, so a sweep is one process and one authority

A proof sweep hands over thousands of lifted words at once, and `verdict -` takes
one word per process. The shell loop that resulted did its own parsing of my
output to recover the letter, which is the shape of a second implementation of
the verdict growing on the caller's side. `vox verdict --tsv <file>` reads
name<TAB>word and writes name<TAB>verdict<TAB>length in input order, with the
tally on stderr. A blank word reports `-` rather than a verdict, since a word
nobody lifted is not a word that failed to close.
