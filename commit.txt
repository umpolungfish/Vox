Pair unbalanced words from the least balance, and expose the pairing

cyclic_pairs gave up when the fork and fuse counts differed and reported every
fork and every fuse unmatched. The surplus lemma says otherwise: read from the
index where the running balance is least and no fuse finds the stack empty, so
the pairing is total on the smaller species and what is left over is the surplus
alone, one side of it empty. On mt-co1 that is six regions answered and eleven
divisions standing, not seventeen and six. Verdicts are unchanged across the
sixty-five word protein sweep and across ls, dash and gzip.

vox pairs <word> prints the pairing: every region with what stands inside it and
whether any of it works, then what is left unanswered or unopened. vox rna takes
--dialect, so a mitochondrial gene reads with UGA as tryptophan from the command
line instead of terminating after twenty-five residues.
