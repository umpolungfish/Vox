Bake the two-ring winding membrane from the auto-designed word

The auto-designer gave the word for the two rings whose relative rotation is the
order: ⊢∈≻⋈⊤≺⊥⋈⊙⊞∋⊡⊣, the orbit ring against the unit ring, the gates building
the ring rather than precomputed cells. ring_membrane realizes it as a baked
binary with the base and modulus compiled in and no runtime input. The fork
opens two arms, the ≻⋈⊤ arm steps multiply-by-a to grow the orbit ring, the
≺⊥⋈⊙ arm holds the unit ring, ⊞ holds them as the interlocked paradox, ∋ fuses
to the relative rotation, and ⊡ fixes the winding. The step count where the
orbit arm returns to the unit is the order, and it closes the factors.

Baked and run: 15 reads rotation 4 and closes 3 x 5, 91 reads 12 and closes
7 x 13, 143 reads 60 and closes 11 x 13. baked_case.py carries a ring mode.
The relative rotation is still walked to closure; reading it below the walk is
the concentration, the next rung on this membrane.
