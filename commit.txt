Fix vox tables: walk from the real function start, not a guessed offset

Ran a sweep of vox tables across the whole factoring toolkit right after
adding it, 106 real functions, 36 flagged. Most read clean, but a handful
(verify_width, mod_inv, eval_at_m, congruence_multilimb) opened with
garbled .byte lines before the real instructions showed up.

The cause was in the display code, not in anything it found: it decoded
raw starting from real_off - 4, and real_off is an offset found in the
NIBBLE-SHIFTED stream. There's no reason that offset lands on a genuine
instruction boundary in the real, correctly aligned bytes, so decoding
cold from there could itself start mid-instruction.

Fixed by walking forward from offset 0 of the function's real bytes,
exactly the way the actual disassembly would, and only printing once the
walk passes the flagged region. Every line is now guaranteed to start on
a real instruction boundary, because it was reached by decoding every
instruction before it, not by guessing where one starts.

Re-verified against all six functions checked in the original sweep,
including the two that were already reading correctly
(is_prime_miller_rabin, gen_prime) to confirm the fix didn't change
anything that was already right.
