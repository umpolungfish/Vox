Audit: fallback sweep of what recursive descent never reached

Recursive descent only follows direct edges, so on a large stripped binary it
claims little (6% of 7z.exe) and the rest, reached only through indirect calls,
went unverdicted. After descent the audit now linearly decodes the unclaimed
executable bytes, groups them into functions of their own at each terminal, and
verdicts those too, the way the Python auditor does. 7z.exe goes from 6% to 99%
covered, 840 functions by descent plus 6662 by sweep, six ill-typed. The corpus
covers 100%. The two are reported separately, so a swept function is never
passed off as a descended one.
